## 1. What is Cybersecurity?
Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting systems, networks, and data from cyberattacks.

---

## 2. CIA Triad (Security Foundation)

### Confidentiality
Protecting information from unauthorized access.

### Integrity
Ensuring data is accurate and not modified without permission.

### Availability
Ensuring systems and services are accessible when needed.

---

## 3. Common Cyber Threats

### Malware
Malicious software designed to harm systems or steal data.

Types of malware:
- Virus
- Worm
- Trojan
- Ransomware
- Spyware

### Phishing
Fraudulent emails or messages that trick users into sharing passwords or sensitive data.

### Brute Force Attack
Trying multiple password combinations to gain access.

### Social Engineering
Manipulating people to reveal confidential information.

---

## 4. Security Tools (Basic Knowledge)

### Antivirus
Helps detect and remove malware.

### Firewall
Filters incoming and outgoing network traffic based on rules.

### VPN (Virtual Private Network)
Encrypts internet traffic and improves privacy.

---

## 5. Password Security Best Practices
- Use strong passwords (12+ characters)
- Use uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols
- Do not reuse passwords across accounts
- Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)
- Avoid saving passwords on shared systems

---

## 6. Vulnerability vs Exploit

### Vulnerability
A weakness in a system, application, or network.

### Exploit
A method or technique used to take advantage of a vulnerability.

---

## 7. Basic Security Practices
- Keep software updated and patched
- Avoid suspicious links and downloads
- Use antivirus and firewall protection
- Backup important data regularly
- Use strong authentication methods
